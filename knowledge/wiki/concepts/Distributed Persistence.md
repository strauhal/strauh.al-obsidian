# Distributed Persistence

**The recurring conversion of a bounded person into forms that can continue acting,
teaching, speaking, or generating relationships in his absence.**

The cloneable archive, public voice model, speaking brain, teaching maxim, and drawing
club all separate continuation from permanent physical participation. This differs from
simple memorialization: the surviving structure is meant to remain usable and generative.

The tension is [[Human-Machine Tug of War|integrity after transmission]]. Ernest wants
the pattern to travel without losing the human trace that makes it recognizable.
