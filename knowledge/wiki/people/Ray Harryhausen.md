# Ray Harryhausen

**Stop-motion animator and visual effects pioneer (1920–2013); listed among Ernest's influences and given his own line in the sketchbook.**

## Summary
Listed among the [[influences]]. The [[ideas sketchbook 2020 25|sketchbook]] notes,
in a single working line: *"harryhausen glued together the human world and the
model"* — a compact description of exactly what Harryhausen's practical-effects
work does: live actors and hand-built miniatures composited into one believable
frame, the seam between the real and the constructed made to disappear.

## Key points
- The line sits in a list alongside other artists' own signature moves (Degas
  and his dancers, a skater popping an ollie) — Harryhausen's is the splice
  between the world and the model.
- A physical, hand-built precursor to the same problem later technologies
  (compositing, CG, VR) solve digitally — worth reading next to
  [[The Oedipal Screen]]'s fascination with screens dissolving the boundary
  between real and constructed.

## Connections
- [[The Oedipal Screen]] — an earlier, analog version of the same real/constructed seam.
- [[Transmitting My Neural Signals by Hand]] — the model-making itself is a hand-crafted process.
