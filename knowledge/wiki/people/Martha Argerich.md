# Martha Argerich

**Pianist; two of her recordings are in the current listening rotation, and the sketchbook singles her out with a one-line note on interpretation versus creation.**

## Summary
The [[current-cultural-diet|reading/listening list]] carries two Argerich
recordings — *Chopin: Preludes & Piano Sonata No. 2* and *Schumann: Kinderszenen
& Kreisleriana* — among a listening diet otherwise dense with
[[Spontaneity and Elegance|interpreters]] (Richter, Gilels, Lupu, Le Sage). The
[[ideas sketchbook 2020 25|sketchbook]] has a single terse line about her: *"martha
argerich does not build pianos"* — a note on the distinction between the
performer who interprets through an instrument and the maker of the instrument
itself.

## Key points
- The sketchbook line isolates interpretation as its own act, separate from
  original creation/construction — worth reading against the vault's broader
  interest in spontaneous, felt execution over laboured original invention.
- Argerich's own reputation (famously impulsive, live-wire performances) makes
  her a fitting anchor for that specific thought, even in one line.

## Connections
- [[Spontaneity and Elegance]] — an interpreter's spontaneity as its own kind of authorship.
