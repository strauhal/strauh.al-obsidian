# Curated Disorder

**The archive's double requirement: enough taxonomy to remain navigable, enough accident
to let unrelated periods and media disclose new relations.**

The 2026 century-based reorganization increased historical legibility, while the
randomize control preserved the earlier site's anachronistic discovery. Audience feedback
shows that this juxtaposition was part of what readers valued.
