# Personality (PAS)

**Self-administered result from the Personality Assessment System's Indirect Assessment Inventory: primitive type EFA — Externalizer, Flexible, Role Adaptive — with a low Activity Level.**

## Summary
The PAS frames personality along three inherent dimensions, each a spectrum between two
poles, plus a fourth "activity level" measure. Ernest's result:

- **Intellectual dimension: Externalizer (E)**, relative strength 4/20 (a mild lean, not
  a strong one). The Externalizer's "natural frame of reference lies in the world outside
  of himself" — perceptual and concrete rather than abstract, oriented toward doing over
  thinking, finding satisfaction through direct interpersonal relating rather than
  internalized reflection.
- **Procedural dimension: Flexible (F)**, relative strength 18/20 (a strong lean). The
  primitive F is "aware of, and responsive to, a wide variety of stimuli" — ingenious,
  adaptable, original, prone to a great many interests rather than sustained focus, and
  needing to *understand* something before he can learn it (rote learning is difficult).
- **Social dimension: Role Adaptive (A)**, described in the report as "balanced" (meaning
  he can move between Role Adaptive and Role Uniform patterns), read here as Role
  Adaptive: socially imitative, interpersonally responsive, versatile at reading and
  adopting the role a situation calls for — with the system's own caveat that this
  reflects "what the individual appears to be, rather than what he is."
- **Low Activity Level** — the system's own weakest-evidenced measure, associated with
  "more of a tendency towards moodiness... to think about things about which nothing can
  be done," as against a high-activity lean toward action over musing.

## Key points
- The [[Spontaneity and Elegance|EFA]] combination is, per the system's own description,
  "the most involving and relating" of the primitive types — highly responsive to people,
  attractive to others, but with a real cost: fickleness, distractibility, and (per the
  report's own blunt language) a risk of reading as "spoiled" or narcissistic if
  environmental structure doesn't compensate for it.
- This isn't a clinical diagnosis — it's a self-administered indirect-assessment result
  from a specific personality theory (the PAS), read here as one more piece of
  self-documentation, the same epistemic status as the diary or sketchbook.
