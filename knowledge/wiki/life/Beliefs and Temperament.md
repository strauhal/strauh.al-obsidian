# Beliefs and Temperament

**Directly-stated answers on belief, politics, decision-making, temperament, and
relationships — filling gaps the diary and sketchbook leave as inference rather than
plain statement.**

## Summary

**Belief.** "i believe in a god" — theist, no further specification of denomination or
tradition given.

**Politics.** "i am post-left" — stated plainly, as a deliberate choice to have on record
rather than left out of the archive.

**A belief most people around him don't share.** "for things to get better, things need
to get way, way *way* worse."

**Decision-making.** When stuck: intuition, not deliberation or avoidance.

**An irrational fear.** "not making the most out of my humanity."

**Confidence and insecurity — the same domain, both ways.** Confident in art and music;
also quietly insecure in art and music. Not two different domains — the same one, held
both ways at once.

**Disposition.** "i drift through a mix of realism and absurdism" — not a fixed optimist
or pessimist.

**Self-typing.** Either INTJ or INFJ, unresolved ("idk").

**What makes him laugh.** "hyper referential stuff based on lived experiences."

**Conflict style.** Stews on it — for years, by his own account, not weeks.

**Worst habit, in his own accounting.** "i am very aloof, i think too much about *what
could be* instead of *what is*."

**A regret/open curiosity.** Wonders "what a solitary life alone in a big city without
friends or family would be like."

**Proudest moment (outside the diary's own contents).** Not a single event inside the
diary, but the fact of it: deciding to start keeping the diary, and keeping it running as
long as he did.

**Biggest influence on how he thinks**, outside the artists/philosophers already logged
in the archive: his wife.

**On friends.** "some of the people who have hurt me the most have paradoxically helped
me the most" — offered as the one thing worth knowing about that relationship register,
rather than a roster of names.
