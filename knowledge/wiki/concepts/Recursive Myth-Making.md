# Recursive Myth-Making

**The risk that a self-interpretation is summarized, crosslinked, retrieved, repeated,
and re-imported until one source appears to have become many independent confirmations.**

A raw diary sentence can produce a concept note, map entry, chatbot answer, memory
summary, and later analysis. Without derivation tracking, the archive may mistake this
family of descendants for corroboration. Coherence then becomes partly manufactured by
the system observing itself.

## Countermeasures

- Preserve raw sources beside synthesis.
- Mark source type, claim date, confidence, contradictions, retrospective editing, and
  derivation through [[Map - Claim Provenance]].
- Count common descent as one source.
- Keep contradictory versions rather than silently reconciling them.
- Treat generated connections as hypotheses until a person or primary artifact confirms
  them.
