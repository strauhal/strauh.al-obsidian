# Controlled Surrender

**A preferred creative loop: construct a constraint, allow a medium or system to produce
something unpredicted, then recover authorship through selection and revision.**

It links GAN collaboration, pareidolia, improvisation, repetitive line drawing,
randomized archive browsing, and the sketchbook claim that more rules can produce more
freedom. Total obedience feels dead; total surrender threatens the self. Productive
resistance keeps the process alive.
