# Interest-Dependent Persistence

**Uneven persistence across scales: individual containers are often abandoned, while the
larger ecosystem absorbs their remains and continues for years.**

The pattern distinguishes difficulty sustaining arbitrary routines from an inability to
commit. The archive is the stable attractor; projects become temporary research organs
inside it.
