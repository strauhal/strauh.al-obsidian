# Decadence Is Beautiful

**"the american empire is crumbling. but decadence is beautiful" — ornament, artifice, and civilizational rot aestheticized rather than mourned.**

## Summary
The [[ideas sketchbook 2020 25|sketchbook]] states it as a throwaway aside that reads as a
thesis: *"the american empire is crumbling. but decadence is beautiful."* It sits a few
entries from that same sketchbook's "beauty fills the god shaped hole made by science" —
both agree that decline, even disenchantment, can be redeemed by ornament and style rather
than denied. The [[current-cultural-diet|reading list]] names the movement's own
literature — Baudelaire's *Les Fleurs du mal* and Camille Paglia's *Sexual Personae: Art
and Decadence from Nefertiti to Emily Dickinson* — and the [[influences|influences page]]
puts [[Charles Baudelaire]] and [[Egon Schiele]] alongside Kees van Dongen and Otto Dix,
two of decadent/Weimar painting's own house names. Schiele in particular supplies the raw,
anxious, unidealized body the archive's large cluster of figurative and nude imagery keeps
circling back to — not generic erotica, but the same nervous, overripe body Schiele and
the Decadents made their subject.

## Key points
- Decline read as an aesthetic position, not a political complaint: the same collapse
  [[Rebuilding from the Bottom]] treats as wreckage to salvage, this concept savors instead.
- Paglia's *Sexual Personae* is, on the reading list, the closest thing to an actual theory
  of the archive's single largest visual cluster — the body as Western art's oldest site
  for working out beauty and danger together.
- [[Egon Schiele]] is already logged in the [[ideas sketchbook 2020 25|sketchbook]] as a
  working ingredient in Ernest's own figurative style ("combining paul jenkins with egon
  schiele and ilya repin") — the decadent line isn't just admired, it's a recipe.

## Connections
- [[Art Fills the God-Shaped Hole]] — the same re-enchantment move, aimed at decline instead of disenchantment.
- [[Spontaneity and Elegance]] — Schiele's nervous line as decadence's formal signature.
- [[Rebuilding from the Bottom]] — the opposite response to the same premise: salvage instead of savor.
- [[Autofiction]] — Paglia and the Decadents both make the artist's own body/persona the artwork.
- [[Charles Baudelaire]] — the movement's founding poet, already read.
- [[Egon Schiele]] — the movement's body, already an influence.
