# Recognition Through Shared Objects

**A relational pattern in which contact becomes meaningful when another person responds
to a made or collected object with evidence of sustained attention.**

The archive invites strangers to look; computerdrawing.club invites them to make. Bug
reports, drawings, interpretations, and derivative personal websites demonstrate a form
of recognition more specific than a metric or generic compliment. The object mediates
the approach and lets intimacy develop around shared attention.
