## Artist Bio (Short)

Ernest Strauhal is a composer and visual artist based in California. His compositions and visual works have been shown and performed across Illinois, New York, California, and New Mexico. In 2021, Strauhal released his first music project "Beuys 1974" on .phonographiq and his second project "Planes of Intensity" through Tone Deaf Records.

Strauhal has had several of his compositions performed at the historic Grace Place Sanctuary in Chicago (No. 12 and No. 13, Sans titre) and has played selections from his music in the greater Chicago area (Color Club, Cafe Mustache).

## Artist Bio (Long)

Ernest Strauhal is a composer and visual artist based in California. Working across both sound and image, Strauhal's practice moves between computer-assisted composition and visual art, with works shown and performed across Illinois, New York, California, and New Mexico.

In 2021, Strauhal released his first music project, "Beuys 1974," on .phonographiq, followed by his second project, "Planes of Intensity," through Tone Deaf Records. These releases mark the beginning of a body of work grounded in computer-assisted composition.

Strauhal's compositions have been performed live at the historic Grace Place Sanctuary in Chicago, including the pieces No. 12 and No. 13, Sans titre. He has also presented selections from his music throughout the greater Chicago area, including at Color Club and Cafe Mustache, bringing his work outside of traditional concert settings and into the city's broader creative community.
